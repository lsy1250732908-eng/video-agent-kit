# Behavioral review cases

Review with concrete inputs, without paid generation unless authorized:

- Prompt-only request with a reference: deliver wording; no upload or generation.
- Request for another provider: respect it rather than routing to Kling.
- Two subjects inside/outside a glass container: keep spatial layers and counts consistent.
- Fixed-camera request: no contradictory dolly or orbit.
- Music cue: distinguish source, generation and edit clocks; do not guarantee frame accuracy.
- Feedback without a viewable result: label hypotheses; do not claim visual review.
- Existing generated task with failed download: resume download, retain task identity.

These cases are review prompts, not claims of independent model evaluation.
